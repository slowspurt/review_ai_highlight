Project note: a shared reading list

Our team kept book suggestions in several chat threads. I put them in one reading list so we could find them again.

How I approached it

By bringing the team's recommendations into a shared space that connected individual interests with the wider reading process, I made the list part of how we exchanged ideas.

What happened

I added a title, a short note, and a link for each book. Two teammates tried the list and asked for a way to mark what they had already read.

This experience reinforced the importance of creating a shared space where recommendations could be brought together and made available to the team.
